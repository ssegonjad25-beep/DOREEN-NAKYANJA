What Sets Us Apart

Six Reasons Parents Choose Dugnad

Every detail of our school is designed around the safety, growth, and joy of your child.

Child-First Safety

A secure campus, trained staff, and clear protocols ensure your child is safe and cared for every moment of the day.

Blended Curriculum

We combine the national framework with creative, activity-based teaching so learning is both rigorous and joyful.

ICT From the Start

Digital skills are introduced early and built up each year — preparing learners for the world they'll grow into.

Balanced Meals

Nutritious meals and snacks prepared daily, because healthy bodies support active, focused minds.

Safe School Van

Reliable pickup and drop-off with trained drivers — giving working parents peace of mind.

Parent Partnership

Open communication and shared effort — the true spirit of dugnad — mean you're never out of the loop.
