About Us

Growing Together, Learning Together

Built on the Norwegian idea of dugnad — voluntarily working together for a common goal — we partner with parents and the community to raise confident, curious learners.

  • Activity-based curriculum for a holistic child
  • Low teacher-pupil ratio
  • Diversity: local and foreign learners
  • Strong parent–school partnership
