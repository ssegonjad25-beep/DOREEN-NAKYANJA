About Dugnad School

A contemporary, fast-growing Kindergarten and Day Care in Wakiso District — where every child is unique, and we learn by doing.

Who We Are

A Learner-Centered School Built on Dugnad

DUGNAD is a contemporary, fast-growing Kindergarten and Day Care located in Kavule–Jomayi Estate along Matuga–Semuto Road in Wakiso District, just an hour's drive from the capital city, Kampala.

We are dedicated to providing an activity-based curriculum that aims at producing a holistic child through formal and informal Early Childhood learning programs. At DUGNAD, we believe every child is unique and should be treated to bring out their potential.

Through a talented and experienced staff team with a low teacher–pupil ratio, we offer lifelong learning based on foundational skills and focusing on critical thinking.

  • Day Care from 6 months, Kindergarten from 3 years, Primary from 6 years (P1–P4)
  • Blended, learner-centered curriculum with ICT and hands-on learning
  • Diverse, inclusive community — local and foreign learners welcome
  • Balanced diet, child safety, clean environment and school van

Our Philosophy

The Spirit of Dugnad

Dugnad is a Norwegian concept of voluntary, collective work for the common good. It is not charity — it is the understanding that we all benefit when we each contribute our part.

At Dugnad School, this philosophy shapes how learners, teachers, parents and the community work together. We build a school where everyone belongs, everyone contributes, and everyone grows.

That is why our motto is simple: “We Learn by Doing.” Children learn best when they participate, collaborate and take responsibility — just as a community does.
