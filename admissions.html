What You'll Need

Admission Requirements

Documents and details to prepare for a smooth application.

Documents

Please bring originals and copies where possible.

  • Child's birth certificate or copy
  • Immunization / health record
  • Recent passport-size photographs (2–4)
  • Parent / guardian national ID copy
  • Previous school report (P1–P4 transfers only)

Eligibility

Programs are organized by age and readiness.

  • Day Care: Ages 2+
  • Kindergarten: Ages 3–5
  • Primary 1–4: Age-appropriate entry
  • A short informal readiness chat for young learners
  • Commitment to our parent–school partnership
