What We Offer

Holistic Learning Environment

Everything your child needs to thrive — as outlined in our school brochure.

Blended Curriculum

We combine the Ugandan national curriculum (NCDC) with proven international early-learning methods for a well-rounded foundation.

Learner-Centered Approach

Teaching that focuses on the individual needs and pace of each child, with small classes and personal attention.

ICT

Integrating technology into everyday learning for digital literacy — from tablets in Kindergarten to coding in Primary.

Child Safety

A secure, caring environment with vetted staff, a supervised campus, first-aid trained teachers and a clear visitor policy.

Balanced Diet

Nutritious, freshly prepared meals and snacks that support healthy growth. Allergies and dietary needs are accommodated.

Clean Environment

A hygienic, well-maintained campus with daily cleaning, safe play areas and clean water — for healthy learning.

School Van

Safe, reliable pickup and drop-off on scheduled routes, driven by trained staff with child-safety checks.

Frequently Asked

Quick answers to common questions about our programs.

We accept children from 6 months (Day Care) through Primary 4 (approximately 10 years). Each program has a dedicated age band and age-appropriate facilities.

Our van covers scheduled routes around Kavule, Jomayi Estate, Matuga, and Semuto Road. Contact the office to confirm whether your location is on an existing route or can be added.

All staff are vetted and first-aid trained. The campus is supervised throughout the day, visitors must sign in, and our school van follows strict child-safety check procedures at every pickup and drop-off.

We blend the Ugandan national curriculum (NCDC) with proven international early-learning methods. Our learner-centered approach means each child progresses at their own pace with plenty of hands-on activities.

Yes. Our kitchen prepares balanced meals daily and we accommodate documented allergies and dietary requirements. Please inform us during enrollment so we can plan accordingly.
